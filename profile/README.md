# ProHuman Health

**ProHuman Health** is a full-featured clinic management platform built for physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and allied health practices. It brings clinical workflows, operations, and administration into a single, role-aware system.

---

## What it does

### Patient Management
- Unified patient profiles with demographics, complaints, labels, and custom fields
- Document storage — upload referral letters, lab reports, imaging, and clinical notes
- Treatment plan tracking: goals, session counts, progress, and discharge summaries
- Consent management with digital signature capture

### Appointments & Scheduling
- Multi-doctor calendar with availability and slot management
- Session booking, confirmation, and status tracking
- Public appointment request form for walk-ins and new patients
- Waitlist management

### Clinical Sessions
- Structured session records with pre-session notes and form responses
- Custom form builder with question types: free text, scales, yes/no, multiple choice, body maps, and medical media uploads
- Session-level document attachments
- PDF session upload for legacy records

### AI-Assisted Chart Review
- Automatic pre-visit brief generated from the patient's full chart — sessions, forms, treatment plans, and documents
- Free-form Q&A against a patient's record during consultation
- Document content (PDFs, Word files) included in AI context automatically
- Strict session isolation: no data bleeds between patients

### Invoicing & Billing
- Invoice creation, line items, and status tracking
- Cash register with session-linked payment recording

### Staff & Access Control
- Multi-role system: super admin, admin, doctor, front desk, and more
- Granular permission matrix configurable per role via a visual Role Builder
- Staff leave management and admin activity logging

### Communication
- In-app notifications
- WhatsApp message templates and patient messaging
- Internal messaging between staff

### Operations
- Inventory management with stock tracking
- Clinic closed-day configuration
- Branch-aware data scoping for multi-location practices
- Google Calendar integration for doctor schedules
